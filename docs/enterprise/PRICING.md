## FAQ

### Can I migrate from Community to Pro/Enterprise?

Yes. Migration is seamless:
- Your existing CLAUDE.md, skills, and workflows remain intact
- No downtime required
- Pro/Enterprise features activate immediately upon subscription

### Who owns the data generated with Claude Craft?

**You do.** All generated code, artifacts, and configurations are yours. We do not claim any rights to your work.

**Data storage:**
- Community: Local only (no telemetry)
- Pro: Usage analytics stored encrypted (EU data centers, GDPR-compliant)
- Enterprise: Self-hosted option available (zero data leaves your infrastructure)

### What happens if I cancel?

- **Pro/Enterprise → Community:** You keep all features for the remainder of the billing period, then revert to Community tier
- **No lock-in:** Your code, skills, and workflows continue to work (Community features remain available)
- **Export:** Download all analytics data before cancellation (available for 30 days post-cancellation)

### Is there a trial period?

- **Pro:** 14-day free trial (no credit card required)
- **Enterprise:** Custom proof-of-concept (30 days, tailored to your use case)

### Can I pay annually?

Yes. Annual billing offers:
- **Pro:** €490/year (2 months free, €40.83/month effective)
- **Enterprise:** €4,990/year (2 months free, €415.83/month effective)

### Do you offer discounts for non-profits/education?

Yes:
- **Non-profits:** 50% off Pro/Enterprise (verification required)
- **Education:** Free Pro for students/educators (GitHub Student Pack eligible)

### What payment methods do you accept?

- Credit/debit card (Stripe)
- SEPA direct debit (EU)
- Bank transfer (Enterprise only, annual plans)
